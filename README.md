# Thief's Gotta Eat

*Thief's Gotta Eat* is a stealth puzzle game. You are a street urchin trying to make ends meet by robbing a museum of their latest art piece, avoiding night guards, and using mirrors to exploit the museum’s Lightbeam Security System™ to break in and steal your prize.

## Team HMSS
This game was made in 6 weeks by a team of 4 students of U-tad (The University of Technology, Arts, and Design in Madrid):

 * Simon Caballero - Scripter & 3D Modeler
 * Jorge Lopez - Technical Artist, Level Designer & Environment Artist
 * Han Meerholz - Scripter & Level Designer
 * Sofiia Sherman - System & UI Designer & Animator

## Controls
The default controls for the demo games are:
 * WASD to move
 * Ctrl to toggle crouching
 * Shift to toggle sprinting
 * E to interact
 * Q to rotate
 * R to restart the level

## Tools Used
 * This game was developed using [Unity](https://unity.com/) (6000.3.16f1)
 * To create some of our own 3D models, we used [Blender](https://www.blender.org/).
 * For the associated textures, we used [Substance 3D Painter](https://www.adobe.com/products/substance3d/apps/painter.html).
